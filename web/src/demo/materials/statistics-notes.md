## Gamma distribution

The sum of k independent exponential random variables with the same rate follows a Gamma distribution. Its shape is k and its scale is the reciprocal of the rate.

## Central limit theorem

For independent, identically distributed variables with finite variance, the standardized sample mean approaches a normal distribution as the sample size increases.
